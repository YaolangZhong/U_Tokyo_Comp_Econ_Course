Course syllabus

Course direction

The course combines computational economic methods with practical research workflows. Lectures move through the core methods earlier, leaving a closing block for recent computational literature using machine-learning tools. Labs develop students’ use of LLM agents for research, alongside Git and other regular tools. Python supports the computational work.

Topic decks and weekly sessions

The schedule is organized by week, while slides are organized by topic. A topic may occupy part of a week or continue across several weeks. Classes meet on Wednesdays in Period 4, International Academic Research Building, Seminar Room 514.
